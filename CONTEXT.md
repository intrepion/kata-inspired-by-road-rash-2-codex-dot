# Glossary

- **Stage**: A named road race with a fixed finish distance.
- **Circuit**: The three stages in qualification order.
- **Qualification**: Finishing a stage in third place or better.
- **Rider stamina**: A racer's ability to stay on their motorcycle under attack.
- **Bike condition**: The motorcycle's remaining tolerance for crashes.
- **Crash**: Ejection from a motorcycle, followed by a slide and return.
- **Return**: The on-foot journey back to the fallen motorcycle.
- **Remount**: Resuming motorcycle control after reaching the bike.
- **Reach**: The short distance in which a neighboring rider can be attacked.
- **Nitro**: A limited charge that briefly increases maximum speed.
